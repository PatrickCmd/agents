# Banoffee Pie Recipe

An easy family favorite with buttery pastry and sweet dulce de leche. Best served with a generous dollop of cream.

## Ingredients

- 4 bananas, sliced
- 394g caramel or dulce de leche
- 300ml double cream
- Dark chocolate (optional)

### For the Pastry

- 100g chilled butter (plus extra for greasing)
- 200g plain flour
- 1 medium egg, separated
- 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

## Method

1. Make the pastry case by pulsing butter and flour in a food processor until it resembles fresh breadcrumbs. Add the egg yolk and sugar and pulse until mixed. Add cold water a tablespoon at a time until dough comes together.
2. Knead dough gently by hand until smooth. Wrap in cling film and chill for 30 minutes.
3. Heat oven to 190C/170C fan/gas 4. Grease a 23cm loose bottomed fluted tart tin. Roll out pastry and line the tin, leaving about half a cm over the edge.
4. Line base with parchment, weigh down with baking beans or rice. Bake 15 minutes. Remove beans and paper, brush pastry with beaten egg white. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until golden and crisp. Cool completely.
5. Spread half the caramel on the pastry case, layer bananas on top. Cover with remaining caramel and chill.
6. Whip cream until thick and spoon over tart. Garnish with grated dark chocolate if desired.

## Notes

- Can be made a day ahead.
- Optional chocolate garnish adds extra flavor.

Enjoy your delicious Banoffee Pie!
