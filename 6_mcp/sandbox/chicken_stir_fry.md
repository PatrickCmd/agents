# Chicken Stir Fry with Rice Noodles

**Author:** Nagi  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 5 minutes  
**Total Time:** 15 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 150 	6 180 g / 5 	6 6 oz chicken breast, cut into thin strips
- 1 tsp cornflour / cornstarch (optional)
- 1 tsp oil (peanut, vegetable, canola) (optional)

### Sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce (or substitute with Hoisin)
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp each sesame oil, sugar (optional)
- 1 1/2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine (Shaoxing wine) OR Mirin
- 1/4 tsp white pepper (or black pepper)
- 3 tbsp water

### Stir Fry
- 100 g / 3.5 oz dried rice noodles (not listed in extracted ingredients but commonly used)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 1 small onion, sliced (white, brown, yellow)
- 1/2 bunch choy sum or other Asian greens, stems separated from leaves
- 1/2 red capsicum / bell pepper, sliced
- 1 carrot, peeled, cut in half lengthwise and sliced on the diagonal
- 5-7 mushrooms, sliced
- Sesame seeds and finely sliced green onions/scallions (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix chicken, cornflour, and oil in a bowl.
2. Mix sauce ingredients in a bowl.
3. Just before cooking, soak noodles in hot water according to packet directions, then drain.
4. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
5. Add garlic and onion, cook for 30 seconds.
6. Add chicken, cook for 1 minute until it changes colour from pink to white.
7. Add carrot, choy sum stems, and capsicum. Cook for 1 minute.
8. Add mushrooms, cook for another 30 seconds.
9. Add noodles, choy sum leaves, and sauce. Toss for 1 minute until leaves have wilted and sauce has evaporated. Do not toss for more than 1.5 minutes to avoid noodle breakage.
10. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions/scallions, then serve immediately.

---

This is a quick, healthy, and delicious stir fry that is perfect for a midweek meal using everyday ingredients. The key is to cook everything quickly on high heat to keep the chicken juicy and the noodles intact.
