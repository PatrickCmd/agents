# Healthy Chicken, Spinach & Bacon Alfredo Pasta Bake Recipe

## Ingredients
- 6 bacon strips
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt to taste
- 2 tablespoons butter (for sauce)
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 10 oz package fresh spinach
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1.5 cups grated Parmesan cheese (more for garnish)
- 8 oz penne pasta

## Instructions

### Cook Bacon
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C).
2. Line a large baking sheet with aluminum foil or parchment paper.
3. Arrange bacon strips and bake for 15 minutes.
4. Transfer cooked bacon to a paper towel-lined platter to absorb extra grease.
5. Crumble the bacon once it's cool enough.

### Cook Chicken
1. Paper towel dry chicken breasts.
2. Season with Italian seasoning and salt.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in a large deep 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add chicken breasts and cook 3-4 minutes on each side until nicely browned.
5. If the chicken is still pink in the center, reduce heat to medium and cook covered until no longer pink.
6. Remove chicken from skillet and cut into strips.

### Cook Garlic and Spinach
1. Add 2 more tablespoons of butter to the skillet.
2. Add chopped garlic and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add fresh spinach and cook until wilted, about 2 minutes.

### Make Alfredo Sauce
1. Add heavy cream and bring to a boil.
2. Add paprika, salt, and pepper; then reduce to a simmer.
3. Slowly add Parmesan cheese, stirring until melted and sauce thickens.
4. Adjust cheese quantity to preferred thickness.

### Cook Pasta
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Cook pasta according to the package instructions until al dente.
3. Drain pasta (do not rinse).

### Combine Everything
1. Add chicken strips and crumbled bacon into the Alfredo sauce.
2. Season sauce to taste.
3. Add cooked pasta and stir until well coated with sauce.
4. Serve with additional grated Parmesan cheese on top.

## Tips to Lighten Up the Recipe
- Use half-and-half instead of heavy cream.
- Use less cheese (reduce to 1/4 cup and add 1 tablespoon flour to thicken).
- Use less bacon or replace it with fried shallots for flavor.
- Use gluten-free pasta alternatives like red lentil or brown rice penne if desired.
